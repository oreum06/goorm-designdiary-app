Morning at Le Marais

Field Notes

The smell of fresh croissants and dark roasted coffee filling the morning air. We sat outside for hours, watching the city slowly wake up. The cobblestones were still slightly damp from a brief overnight shower, reflecting the warm glow of the streetlamps fading into the dawn.
